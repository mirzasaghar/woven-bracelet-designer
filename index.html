The chart your artisans weave from

Drawn from the same bead grid as the preview, so what the customer sees is what gets made. Row 1 is the left end of the band. For a Traditional every other row is drawn half a bead lower, the same as the net. The colour names are stand-ins, your own bead list would replace them.

    What Shopify receives with the order

    These are line item properties on the cart. Anything starting with an underscore is hidden from the customer at checkout but still shows on the order, so the full pattern goes with it.

    Bead colours are data
    Type a name and pick a colour, and it's added to every colour row, the 3D preview and the chart. The shading is worked out from that one colour, there are no images to make and no code to change.
      It takes on your theme
      As an app block, the designer reads the theme's colours and type, so it matches the rest of the store. Try a darker theme.
